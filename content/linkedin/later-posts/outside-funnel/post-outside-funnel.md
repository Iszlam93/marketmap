## Ready-to-publish copy

One question came up while building MarketMap:

What should happen when a marketing project doesn’t belong in TOFU, MOFU or BOFU?

Research, reporting, operations, retention and enablement can support an entire journey without representing one conversion stage.

Forcing them into a funnel gives leadership a misleading picture.

Hiding them makes a large part of the team’s work invisible.

Our current solution is an “Outside funnel” area connected to each journey.

It keeps the funnel meaningful while showing the important work surrounding it.

But this is still a product hypothesis.

How do you represent these activities in your current marketing plan?
