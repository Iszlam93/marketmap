# MarketMap — First 3 LinkedIn Posts

This folder contains the ready-to-publish copy and matching visual designs for the first MarketMap build-in-public series.

## Recommended publishing order

1. `post-01-origin-story.md` — publish first to validate the core problem.
2. `post-02-outside-funnel.md` — publish 3–4 days later to demonstrate product thinking.
3. `post-03-testing-invitation.md` — publish 3–5 days later to recruit early testers.

## Publishing checklist

- Read the post aloud and make small changes so it sounds like your natural voice.
- Use the matching `FINAL.png` as the post image.
- Do not add a public product link to Posts 1 or 2.
- For Post 3, ask people to comment `MAP` or send a direct message until the public testing environment is ready.
- Reply to every relevant comment with a real question about the marketer’s current planning process.
- Record useful feedback in the product discovery notes, not only in LinkedIn comments.

## What to measure

Prioritise meaningful conversations over impressions or likes:

- Marketers confirming that they experience the problem.
- People explaining or showing their current planning setup.
- Early-access requests from relevant marketers.
- Testers willing to map 5–10 real projects.
- Product insights that change or strengthen a product decision.
