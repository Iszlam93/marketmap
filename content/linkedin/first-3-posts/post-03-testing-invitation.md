# Post 3 — Early Testing Invitation

## Purpose

Recruit a small group of relevant marketers for guided prototype testing.

## Visual

Use `designs/post-03-testing-invitation-FINAL.png`.

Alt text: A visual marketing-planning workspace with project cards being placed across three stages and a timeline, inviting marketers to test the prototype.

## Ready-to-publish copy

MarketMap is now at the point where screenshots are no longer enough.

I want to see what happens when marketers use it with real work.

I’m looking for a small number of marketers to:

- Add 5–10 current projects.
- Place them across their journeys.
- Check their funnel coverage.
- See where those projects fall across the year.

This is an early prototype — not a finished product.

I’m specifically looking for the confusing parts, missing information and assumptions we got wrong.

If you work in demand generation or marketing planning and would like to test it, comment **MAP** or send me a message.

I’ll share what we learn in the next update.

## Suggested first comment

The test is deliberately small: five to ten real projects and approximately 20 minutes. You do not need to prepare a polished marketing plan.

## Direct message for interested testers

Thanks for offering to test MarketMap.

It is an early visual planning prototype designed to help marketers see projects across journeys, funnel stages and the year.

I’d like you to try it with 5–10 real or anonymised projects. I’m interested in what feels useful, what is confusing and what is missing — not whether you can complete everything perfectly.

I’ll send you the access details and a short testing task. Would you also be open to a brief follow-up conversation after trying it?

## Publishing notes

- Use `Comment MAP` rather than a public URL until the public testing environment is ready.
- Contact relevant respondents personally; do not send a generic mass reply.
- Ask testers not to upload confidential company information during the early prototype stage.
- Track how many people request access, complete the exercise and agree to a follow-up conversation.
